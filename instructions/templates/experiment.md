# Experiment · Week NN

State whether the work is a toy demonstration, controlled comparison, or replication. Write the hypothesis, baseline, independent and dependent variables, controls, case selection, and sample-size rationale.

Record the data source, availability dates where relevant, model and software versions, parameters, seeds or deterministic enumeration, and reproduction steps. Define metrics and the analysis plan, including uncertainty when the design supports it.

Present measured results, errors and confounders, limitations, and interpretation. Mark a proposed experiment as planned until it runs. Distinguish invariants and predicted outcomes from measured results.
