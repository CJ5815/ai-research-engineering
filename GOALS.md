# Program goals

Over 20 weeks, build the ability to derive core AI algorithms, implement and evaluate LLM systems, and communicate the implications for investment research and decision-making. Budget 6–8 hours per week, with seven hours as the default.

- **Model science:** Derive and implement a perceptron, a small neural network, and a causal decoder. Trace the shapes, numerical behavior, and failure modes.
- **Evidence and decisions:** Compare retrieval and reasoning methods on defined benchmarks. Measure grounding, calibration, and sensitivity to the information available at decision time.
- **Systems engineering:** Build a bounded, observable research workflow with typed tools, verification, and human review.
- **Research and communication:** Run controlled comparisons, document uncertainty, and explain a result to technical and executive audiences.

The [curriculum](docs/curriculum.md) defines the sequence. The [assessment rubric](docs/assessments/index.md) defines observable competency. The [progress record](PROGRESS.md) tracks learner evidence.
