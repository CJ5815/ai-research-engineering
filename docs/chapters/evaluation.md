# Evaluation and testing

Retrieval recall@k is the fraction of relevant items retrieved among the top k. Exact match scores answer correctness; evidence entailment checks grounding. Use held-out questions, fixed budgets, paired comparisons and confidence intervals. For agents also measure tool failures, termination and cost. Treat document contents as untrusted data.

Derive the central equation or algorithm, implement a minimal baseline, and record a test that could falsify your hypothesis.
