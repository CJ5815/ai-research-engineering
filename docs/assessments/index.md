# Assessments

Start with the [Week 1 entry diagnostic and practice bank](week-01.md). Attempt the required subset before requesting hints or opening the separate answer key under `instructions/solutions/` in the local repository.

| Level | Observable evidence |
|---|---|
| 0 · Unassessed | An assessment is pending. |
| 1 · Awareness | Recognize a concept and name a relevant use. |
| 2 · Conceptual understanding | Explain the mechanism and a limitation in your own words. |
| 3 · Mathematical understanding | Derive the expression or trace a new numerical case and check the result. |
| 4 · Implementation | Implement or adapt the method independently and pass acceptance checks on unfamiliar inputs. |
| 5 · Evaluation and troubleshooting | Design a controlled comparison and diagnose a failure from measured evidence. |
| 6 · Design and innovation | Defend a design under constraints and propose a test that could disconfirm its advantage. |
| 7 · Teaching and thought leadership | Teach the method or publish a synthesis with reproducible evidence and justified claims. |

The [Week 2 practice bank](week-02.md) checks the chain rule, array shapes, and independent gradient verification. Assess each competency separately at the checkpoints below.

## Record your evidence

The [syllabus](../curriculum.md) shows which modules are ready. Track your own learning in the repository’s `PROGRESS.md` or in a private Obsidian note. Record the objective, date, independent contribution, evidence location, competency level, and next gap to address.

Mark **studied** after you can summarize the derivation or reading. Mark **practiced** after running or adapting the lab. Mark **demonstrated** only when you can explain, implement, or evaluate the objective on a new input or under a changed assumption. Keep private reflections and assessment artifacts in Obsidian.
