# AI Research & Engineering

Work through a 20-week course in mathematical foundations, LLM engineering, and evidence-grounded investment research. Each week combines assigned reading, derivations, a local notebook, an experiment, and an assessment.

[Start Week 1](weeks/week-01.md) · [Continue to Week 2](weeks/week-02.md) · [View the 20-week syllabus](curriculum.md)

## Course phases

| Weeks | What you’ll learn to do |
|---|---|
| [1–5 · Model foundations](curriculum.md#weeks-15-model-foundations) | Derive gradients, train a small network, and trace a causal decoder. |
| [6–10 · Language models and evidence](curriculum.md#weeks-610-language-models-and-evidence) | Extract structured facts and evaluate retrieval and cited answers. |
| [11–15 · Reasoning, decisions, and tools](curriculum.md#weeks-1115-reasoning-decisions-and-tools) | Check plans, update forecasts, and compare bounded tool workflows. |
| [16–20 · Harnesses, systems, and evaluation](curriculum.md#weeks-1620-harnesses-systems-and-evaluation) | Instrument an agent and defend the results of a held-out evaluation. |

## How to study

Open a ready week from the syllabus. Use the assigned section of the [AI Foundations and Reading Guide](chapters/ai-foundations-and-reading-guide.md), run the core notebook, and assess your work against the [competency rubric](assessments/index.md). Budget 6–8 hours a week and record a reproducible result, a limitation, and your next question.
